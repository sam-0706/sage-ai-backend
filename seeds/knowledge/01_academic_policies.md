# Demo University Student Handbook — Academic Policies (SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT)

This handbook is synthetic demonstration content for the SAGE AI beta. It is not the policy of any real institution.

## Attendance requirement
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course, calculated separately for lectures and tutorials. Attendance is updated on the student portal every Monday. Students below 75% at the end of the term may be barred from the end-term examination for that course unless an attendance condonation is approved.

## Attendance warnings
Students are warned by the Academic Office when their attendance in any course falls below 80% (early warning) and again below 75% (shortage). A shortage warning is not a final decision; it is a prompt to act early by speaking to the course faculty or the Academic Advisor.

## Attendance condonation for medical or approved reasons
Up to 10% attendance may be condoned for documented medical reasons, representation of the university, or family emergencies. To apply, submit the Condonation Request Form on the student portal with supporting documents (for medical absence: a doctor's certificate and, where applicable, a hospital discharge summary). Requests must be submitted within 15 days of returning to classes. The Academic Office decides within 7 working days. Condoned sessions are marked as "authorised absence".

## Section change requests
If a scheduled lecture conflicts with another academic commitment or an approved campus job, a student may request a section change for that course through the Course Coordinator. Requests are accepted during the first six weeks of the term and are subject to seat availability. Students should first check the timetable for alternative sections, then email the Course Coordinator with the course code, current section, requested section and the reason.

## Assignments and late submission
Assignments submitted late lose 10% of the marks per day for up to three days; after three days they are not accepted unless an extension was approved in advance by the course faculty. Extension requests should be made before the deadline.

## Examinations
The end-term examination carries 50% of the course grade unless the course outline says otherwise. The minimum passing mark is 40% overall. Students who fail may appear for a supplementary examination held within six weeks of results.

## Re-evaluation
Students may apply for re-evaluation of an end-term answer script within 7 days of results by paying a re-evaluation fee of INR 500 per course through the portal.

## Academic integrity
Plagiarism and unauthorised collaboration are serious offences handled by the Academic Integrity Committee. When unsure whether collaboration is allowed, ask the course faculty before submitting.
