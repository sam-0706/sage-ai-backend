# Demo University — Fees, Registration, Placements and Clubs (SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT)

This is synthetic demonstration content for the SAGE AI beta.

## Fee payment
Term fees are due by the 10th of the first month of each term. A late fee of INR 1,000 applies after the due date, and registration is blocked if fees remain unpaid 30 days after the due date. Students facing financial difficulty can apply to the Finance Office for an instalment plan before the due date.

## Scholarships
Merit and need-based scholarships are announced at the start of each academic year. Applications require the previous term's grade sheet, family income proof (for need-based), and a statement of purpose.

## Course registration
Course registration opens two weeks before the term starts. Electives are allocated first-come-first-served subject to prerequisites. Students should check prerequisites in the course catalogue before registering.

## Documents and certificates
Bonafide certificates, transcripts and fee receipts can be requested through the portal under "Documents". Standard processing time is 3 working days.

## Placement process
The Placement Cell runs internship and final placement drives. Students must register on the placement portal, upload a resume approved by a Placement Advisor, and attend the pre-placement training. Students can book mock interviews and resume reviews with Placement Advisors through the portal.

## Placement readiness
Placement Advisors recommend that students targeting product and analytics roles build at least one project that shows data analysis, complete a case-interview practice set, and prepare a one-page resume tailored to each role.

## Clubs and opportunities
Clubs announce workshops, competitions and conferences on the student events board. Students can subscribe to categories such as Finance, Marketing, Product, Entrepreneurship and Analytics to receive only relevant announcements.
