# Demo University — Student Support, Tutorials and Office Hours (SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT)

This is synthetic demonstration content for the SAGE AI beta.

## Office hours
Every course faculty holds at least two office hours per week, listed in the course outline and on the faculty directory. Students do not need an appointment during office hours. Outside office hours, email the faculty with the course code in the subject line.

## Peer tutoring and the Learning Centre
The Learning Centre runs free peer tutoring for quantitative courses (Business Statistics, Financial Accounting, Economics) every weekday from 4 pm to 7 pm in Library Room 204. Students can also book 30-minute one-to-one sessions on the student portal under "Learning Centre".

## Business Statistics support
For Business Statistics, the course runs a weekly problem-solving tutorial on Fridays at 2 pm. Recorded tutorial walkthroughs for hypothesis testing, confidence intervals and regression are available in the course LMS page under "Recovery resources".

## Academic Advisor
Each student is assigned an Academic Advisor who can help with workload planning, recovery plans across courses, and academic standing. Advisors can be booked through the portal under "Advising".

## Wellbeing and counselling
The Student Wellbeing Centre offers confidential counselling. Walk-in hours are 10 am to 5 pm on weekdays. In an emergency, contact campus security or call the national emergency number 112. In India, Tele-MANAS offers free 24x7 mental-health support at 14416.

## Health centre
The campus health centre is open 24 hours. Students recovering from illness can obtain a fitness certificate here, which is required when submitting a medical condonation request.

## Accessibility support
Students with a documented disability or long-term health condition can register with the Accessibility Office for accommodations such as extra exam time.
