# SAGE AI Guides for Working Professionals and Founders

General guidance written by the SAGE AI team. It is not institution policy.

## Protecting learning time while working
Block two to three fixed learning slots per week in your calendar and treat them like meetings. Tie each slot to one concrete output (a finished lesson, a practice problem set, a portfolio section). When work spikes, shrink the slot instead of cancelling it.

## Preparing for a role change
Map the target role's job descriptions to your current skills and list the three biggest gaps. For each gap, pick one learning resource and one visible artifact that proves the skill, such as a case study, a dashboard, or a small project. Ask your manager for one stretch assignment that exercises the target skill.

## Manager conversation checklist
State the goal, how it benefits the team, what you are asking for (time, a project, feedback), and a date to review progress. Bring evidence of progress rather than intentions.

## Interview preparation plan
Three weeks before an interview: research the company and role, prepare five stories using the situation-task-action-result format, and do at least two mock interviews. The final week should focus on practice, not new material.

## Founder weekly operating rhythm
Each week, set no more than three outcomes, at least one of which involves talking to customers. Review last week's commitments honestly before setting new ones. Keep a decision log with the evidence behind each decision.

## Customer discovery
Aim for 10 to 15 customer conversations before committing to a pricing model. Ask about past behaviour and current spending rather than hypothetical willingness to pay. Record the exact words customers use to describe the problem.

## Pitch preparation
A pre-seed pitch should explain the problem, why now, the solution, early evidence (customers, usage, revenue), the team, and the ask. Rehearse the pitch at least five times with people who will interrupt with questions.
